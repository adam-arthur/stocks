# Income Portfolio Research

A system for income-focused investors to regularly screen and review their portfolios, understand risks, and explore investment alternatives.

## Goals

- Identify potential weaknesses and vulnerabilities in portfolio holdings.
- Surface investments with similar yields that could improve quality, diversification, or portfolio resilience.
- Provide concise, factual information about income-oriented securities.

Coverage includes dividend-paying stocks, equity REITs, mortgage REITs, business development companies (BDCs), closed end funds (CEFs), preferred securities, bonds, and other fixed-income instruments.

The system presents data and tradeoffs neutrally, helping investors make their own decisions without prescribing a single investment strategy.
