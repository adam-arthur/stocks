Use company-reported AFFO, including reported FAD or CAD where the company's definition has been reviewed and is comparable to AFFO. Do not reconstruct AFFO from FFO and individual adjustments. Prefer reported per-share values; otherwise, calculate them only when the reported total, any required dilution adjustments, and the matching weighted-average diluted shares and OP units are available for the same period and ownership basis. Leave unsupported or ambiguous values empty while retaining available reported totals. Classify accepted reported measures as `Reported` even when per-share values are calculated; use `Unavailable` when no qualifying measure is available and `Unreviewed` until assessed. Do not use a `Calculated` support category.
