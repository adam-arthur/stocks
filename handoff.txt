Implemented equity REIT AFFO yield timeseries in crates/derive/src/valuation.rs. Uses four consecutive quarters of AFFO/share, adjusts for splits, emits daily yields and sparse TTM values at change points, and errors on missing required inputs. Updates take effect on the first trading date after publication. No historical fair-value baseline or swap scoring yet.

Added a shared deterministic publication-date parser in crates/reit-metrics/src/publication.rs. Reads the announcement date from the parent 8-K's Item 2.02, including combined 2.02/7.01 headings, and uses Chrono for date conversion. Applies that date to metrics from the same filing's exhibits; no LLM or company-specific date rules. Preserves published_on through selection, calculated quarters, and ingestion storage. Missing or ambiguous dates for selected metrics cause errors, with no filing-date fallback. Existing quarters without dates trigger backfill on ingestion.

Validation: workspace cargo check and builds passed. Sampled 271 filings across 136 REITs: 260 parsed; 11 returned missing-date errors. Integration checks confirmed date propagation and publication-based yield timing, including errors when publication dates are absent. No unit tests added.
